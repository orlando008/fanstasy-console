# Clockwork Dominion: the story, told in the log in Marco's words. One line each: `trigger | text`.
# Triggers: `name:n` when the run's number reaches n (made: cogs made, sold: cogs sold, coin: coin in hand, tinkers,
# sign, spools: spools of brass bought), or `ev:name` when the code raises the event. book.py packs this into the ROM.

# --- Act I: the Workshop. The first minutes ---
ev:start | Click. Whirr. A warm light, and a face with spectacles. "There you are," says the old man. "Make a cog, little one."
made:1 | I made a cog. It is round, and it has twelve teeth. Paulo holds it up to the lamp and smiles.
made:5 | Five cogs. Paulo says each tooth must be the same as the last. I make them the same.
made:20 | "We will put them in the window," says Paulo. "The town is always needing cogs."
sold:1 | A girl with a broken music box bought a cog. Paulo put the coin in a jar marked MARCO.
sold:25 | The jar is getting heavier. Paulo taps it and says I am paying my way.
ev:nobrass | There is no more brass on the bench. The merchant's cart comes down the hill on market days.
spools:1 | I bought a spool of brass from the merchant. He looked at me for a long time before he took the coin.
ev:priceup | I raised my price. Paulo peers over his spectacles and says nothing, which means: careful.
ev:pricedown | I lowered my price. More hands reach for the window.
ev:unsold | The window is full of cogs that no one buys. Paulo says a price is a kind of question.
coin:50 | Fifty lire in the jar. Paulo says that is more than the shop made all of last winter.
sign:2 | Paulo painted a new sign: BENEDETTO & MARCO, FINE COGS. My name is on it. I read it eleven times.
sign:4 | People come from the next valley now. They point at the sign, and then at me.
tinkers:1 | I wound up a tinker of my own. It is small and it hums as it works. I think it likes me.
tinkers:5 | Five tinkers tick on the bench. Paulo has to keep his tea on the windowsill.
tinkers:12 | The tinkers have learned to pass the brass along in a line. I did not teach them that.
made:500 | Five hundred cogs. Paulo says the clock in the square has never kept such good time.
made:1500 | Paulo sleeps in his chair in the afternoons now. I make the cogs quietly.
