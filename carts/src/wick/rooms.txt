# Wick's rooms. A room is a line `@ world-number | par seconds | its name` (and `| chase` for a room where the Snuffer
# follows him) and 13 rows of 16 cells:
#   .  air        #  stone       -  a ledge (solid from above only)      ~  water (snuffs Wick out)
#   S  the start  L  the lantern (the goal)      *  an ember
# and the hazards (spec section 7; all on one clock of 120 frames, each a quarter of it later for each column along):
#   d  a spout in the stone: a drop falls from it          ^ > <  a steam jet, 3 cells up, right or left: on half the time
#   =  a shelf that crumbles a moment after he lands on it  H V    a gear that moves 3 cells right and back, or up and back
#   P  a pendulum's pivot (in the stone above): its weight swings on a 3-cell arm
#   ( )  wind, blowing left or right                       r      a grate in the stone: rain falls from it, most of the time
# rooms.py packs them into rom/rooms.bin and checks each (one start, one lantern, 13 x 16). Ten rooms and a chase room for each of the four
# worlds (the Dungeon, the Laboratory, the Clock Tower, the Rooftops), then the Chimney's five (world 5).
